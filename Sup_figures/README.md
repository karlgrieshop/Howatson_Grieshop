# Supplementary figures

The tracked figures are `FigS1`–`FigS9`. Their generating scripts are in
`Sup_plotting_and_analyses/`, except Fig. S1 and Fig. S4, which are generated
by scripts in `Plotting_and_analyses/`.

- **S1:** Candidate-gene enrichment.
- **S2:** SA effects by whole-body sex-biased expression.
- **S3:** SA effects in SSDOs and published candidate sets.
- **S4:** SA-effect candidate-class comparison.
- **S5–S6:** Expression and population-genetic metrics.
- **S7:** Fly–human protein conservation.
- **S8:** SA effects adjusted for measured conservation.
- **S9:** SA-resolution stages.
