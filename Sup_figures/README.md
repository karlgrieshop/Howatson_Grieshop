# Supplementary figures

The tracked supplementary manuscript figures are:

- `FigS1_SA_Candidate_Enrichment.png`
- `FigS2_sa_mag.png`
- `FigS3_sa_mag_context.png`
- `FigS4_SA_Magnitude_Brigos5.png`

- `FigS6_rlns_tajD_DoS.png`
- `FigS7_resolution_stages.png`
- `FigS8_fly_human_conservation.png`

Fig1, FigS2, FigS3, and FigS4 each show the retained SA-effect metric as a single unlettered panel.

FigS3 includes SSDOs and candidate sets from Innocenti and Morrow (2010), Grieshop et al. (2025), and Ruzicka et al. (2019), in that order; all estimates are per-gene maximum Ruzicka Wald statistics.

FigS7 compares Penultimate (MB or TS) and Ultimate (HMB or HTS) stages within SSDOs, disease orthologues, orthologues, and background: rMF in panel A and SA effects in panel B. Grey lines show the all-inclusive protein-coding mean for each metric's available-gene universe, without stage filtering. The previous radar overlay is archived in `side/archived_figS7_radar_context/`.

FigS8 compares fly–human whole-alignment protein identity and fly/human protein coverage across SSDOs, disease orthologues excluding SSDOs, and orthologues excluding disease genes. It is generated from a completed, explicitly selected HPC alignment directory by `Sup_plotting_and_analyses/figureS8_fly_human_conservation.R`. Mapping exclusions and the eligible/measured counts are retained in the corresponding `Data/Summary_tables/FigS8_*.csv` exports. Until the requested mapping review is complete, the existing 8,435-gene results are provisional.

Each figure is regenerated by the active scripts in `Plotting_and_analyses/` or `Sup_plotting_and_analyses/`, as described in the top-level `README.md`.

FigS1 asterisks indicate BH-adjusted two-sided Fisher P < 0.05, with adjustment across all nine TableS1 tests.
