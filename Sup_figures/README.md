Fig1, FigS2, FigS3, and FigS4 each show the SA-effect metric as a single unlettered panel. All active SA-effect analyses use `max(abs(Wald_Stat))` over finite SNP statistics overlapping each protein-coding gene, with absolute values taken before the gene maximum and no squaring.

FigS3 includes SSDOs and candidate sets from Innocenti and Morrow (2010), Grieshop et al. (2025), and Ruzicka et al. (2019), in that order; all estimates are per-gene maximum absolute Ruzicka Wald z-statistics.

FigS7 compares Penultimate (MB or TS) and Ultimate (HMB or HTS) stages within SSDOs, disease orthologues, orthologues, and background: rMF in panel A and SA effects in panel B. Grey lines show the all-inclusive protein-coding mean for each metric's available-gene universe, without stage filtering. The previous radar overlay is archived in `side/archived_figS7_radar_context/`.

FigS8 compares fly–human whole-alignment protein identity and fly/human protein coverage across SSDOs, disease orthologues excluding SSDOs, and orthologues excluding disease genes. It is generated from explicitly selected mapping and alignment directories by `Sup_plotting_and_analyses/figureS8_fly_human_conservation.R`. Mapping exclusions and eligible/measured counts are retained in the corresponding `Data/Summary_tables/FigS8_*.csv` exports.

FigS9 compares mean SA effects (`max(abs(Wald_Stat))` per gene) before/after linear adjustment for identity and both coverage measures, on identical measured genes across all orthologues with SA/conservation data. It has one panel, with no one-to-one filter. Whiskers are 95% human-partner-cluster bootstrap intervals. Background has undefined conservation and is excluded. Its `FigS9_*.csv` tables retain the global and disease-only-versus-other regression tests, audit, estimates, and diagnostics.

Each figure is regenerated by the active scripts in `Plotting_and_analyses/` or `Sup_plotting_and_analyses/`, as described in the top-level `README.md`.

FigS1 asterisks indicate BH-adjusted two-sided Fisher P < 0.05, with adjustment across all nine TableS1 tests.
